# Research Evidence Register

This document records research leads and explains how evidence should be used.

Industry statistics are context. They are not measurements from the M5 dataset and must not be presented as predicted savings or project baselines.

## Evidence rules

For every statistic, record:

- Exact claim.
- Value or range.
- Year.
- Geography and industry.
- Sample or study scope.
- Source and direct link.
- Limitation.

Before using a source in a formal report, verify its full bibliographic details and the context of the number.

## Responsible use

Do not turn a broad industry estimate into a claim about this project. Do not report stockout cost, overstock cost, inventory turnover, service level, or forecast accuracy benchmarks without stating the metric definition, period, scope, and source.
