# Project Personas

These personas are design hypotheses. They are not verified stakeholder requirements.

## Meera

Meera is a demand or inventory planner. She reviews products and stores and decides where more investigation is needed.

### Meera needs to

- Filter by store and item.
- View historical sales and the next 28 forecasted days.
- See forecast uncertainty.
- Identify intermittent or low-demand patterns.
- Understand that a zero-sales day may have more than one explanation.
- Review large forecast errors and unusual demand changes.

### Meera must not be shown

A forecast-based attention flag must not be presented as proof of a stockout or overstock situation.

## Rahul

Rahul is a supply-chain or business manager. He reviews performance at a higher level and needs evidence that the forecasting process is reliable.

### Rahul needs to

- Compare WAPE, bias, and interval coverage by store and department.
- Review performance by demand volume and intermittency.
- Identify locations or categories that require attention.
- Download a dated report.
- See the source-data version, forecast-run identifier, model version, and limitations.
- Understand whether an overall improvement hides weak performance for important smaller series.

## Open stakeholder questions

Before the project is treated as an operational solution, confirm:

- Whether Meera and Rahul represent real users.
- Their reporting frequency.
- Their required planning levels.
- Their approval and override responsibilities.
- Their definition of a useful alert.
